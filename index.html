About this activity

Story Hunt is a warm up for the class on covering AI as a beat, where the questions are sources, access, verification, and the risk of regulatory capture.

The topics are deliberately mixed. Some are AI stories, some are not. The point is that the same reading habits apply everywhere: find the primary document, work out who benefits from how it is worded, and check what has already been written before you claim an angle.

What you hand in

A beat, a topic, your prompt check answers, three verified sources with a reason for each, and a note on the workflow you used. Post it to the Padlet when you are done.

Rules

Work in pairs. Run the same prompt through an AI overview, a search engine, a chatbot and an agent. Every link gets opened and checked by a human before it goes on the list. If a source turns out not to exist, that is a finding worth telling the class about.
